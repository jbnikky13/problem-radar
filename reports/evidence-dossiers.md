## 1. Trust / verification of official opportunities and claims

**Current status: corroborated pattern, not yet quantified as a market.**

The repository's cluster data contains repeated recruitment-fraud/false-notice signals. Independent current Nigerian government sources also show the same underlying failure mode: FCSC warned in August 2026 about unauthorised recruitment links and explicitly instructed applicants to verify through official channels; SON issued a September 2026 warning about fake recruitment announcements; and the Police Service Commission has separately warned applicants about recruitment scams. citeturn0search3turn0search6turn0search15

**What is evidenced:** people are exposed to unauthorised recruitment information and agencies repeatedly need to publish corrective warnings.

**What is not yet evidenced:** a defensible national incidence rate, total financial loss, or willingness to pay for a third-party verification product.

**Potential intelligence buyer:** public agencies, recruitment platforms, employers, fraud/risk teams, financial institutions.

**Intelligence product hypothesis:** aggregated monitoring of recurring impersonation/false-notice patterns, with official-source verification and trend alerts.

**Next validation:** count independent official warnings over time; map affected service categories; measure time between false notice circulation and official correction; interview agencies about monitoring costs.

## 2. Service reliability / complaint resolution

**Current status: strongly corroborated operational problem; commercial opportunity still requires buyer validation.**

The repository contains telecom reliability/complaint clusters. NCC's current consumer guidance documents call drops, poor service quality, billing issues and complaint resolution as recurring consumer issues, and its 2026 compensation framework covers prolonged or repeated poor network quality in affected LGAs. The framework also requires operators to identify eligible affected subscribers using network performance data. citeturn0search0turn0search1turn0search4

**What is evidenced:** service-quality failures are measurable, regulated and linked to formal complaint/compensation workflows.

**What is not yet evidenced:** whether an external intelligence provider can create enough incremental value beyond operators' and NCC's existing monitoring.

**Potential intelligence buyers:** telecom operators, regulators, enterprise customer-experience teams, consumer advocacy organisations.

**Intelligence product hypothesis:** independent service-friction intelligence combining public complaints, regulatory events and geographic/service-category trends.

**Next validation:** determine what data operators/regulators already possess, identify blind spots, and test whether an external aggregated signal predicts complaint volume or compensation events.

## 3. Electricity service reliability and customer experience

**Current status: corroborated infrastructure problem; needs customer-level evidence before commercial conclusions.**

The repository contains repeated power-outage clusters. NERC's August 2026 operational factsheet reports average available generation capacity of 4,758 MW and an average load factor of 86%; NERC also publishes recurring operational and metering factsheets. Its May/June 2026 metering report says national metering reached 61.51% by June, with 7,743,839 metered customers out of 12,589,486 active customers. citeturn0search7turn0search10

**What is evidenced:** electricity reliability and customer metering are continuously monitored at national/sector level.

**What is not yet evidenced:** the exact customer pain, regional service differences and willingness of utilities, businesses or government to pay for an additional intelligence layer.

**Potential intelligence buyers:** DisCos, regulators, energy-service companies, large businesses, insurers and infrastructure planners.

**Intelligence product hypothesis:** location/time-aware service-reliability intelligence that joins public operational data with aggregated customer-reported friction.

**Next validation:** compare public operational metrics with independent complaint signals and identify information gaps that existing dashboards do not answer.

## 4. Counterfeit / authenticity verification

**Current status: corroborated risk signal; product scope remains open.**

The repository contains counterfeit-product clusters. NAFDAC's 2024–2027 strategic plan explicitly tracks substandard and falsified/counterfeit medical products and sets inspection/compliance targets through 2027. citeturn0search48

**What is evidenced:** counterfeit/substandard products are an established regulatory concern.

**What is not yet evidenced:** whether a broad consumer verification service would outperform existing regulatory/product-registration systems.

**Potential intelligence buyers:** manufacturers, distributors, pharmacies, retailers, regulators and insurers.

**Intelligence product hypothesis:** aggregated counterfeit-risk and verification intelligence by product category, market and incident type, without exposing complainant identities.

**Next validation:** map existing verification mechanisms and identify categories where verification is still fragmented or difficult.

## Cross-pattern finding

The most credible common mechanism currently visible is **verification + service reliability + information asymmetry**. However, these should not yet be collapsed into one product. The evidence supports investigating the common mechanism, not claiming that one universal solution exists.

## Commercial rule

Problem Radar should sell **aggregated intelligence, provenance, confidence, trends and decision support**. It should not sell names, contact lists, identifiable complaints, private messages or individual-level behavioural profiles.
