# Problem Radar — Buyer Discovery Pack v1

**Pilot:** Information Integrity Intelligence
**Market:** Nigeria
**Commercial principle:** Sell intelligence, not identities.

## Why this pilot now

Several Nigerian public institutions have recently warned about false recruitment, impersonation and AI-generated employment communications. FCSC warned on 21 August 2026 about unauthorised recruitment links; NUPRC warned on 11 August 2026 about fake and AI-generated employment letters; and OSGF currently publishes a warning about fake appointments and impersonation. citeturn0search0turn0search1turn0search2

## Initial buyer targets

### 1. Federal Civil Service Commission (FCSC)
**Why relevant:** It has directly documented unauthorised recruitment links and provides official verification guidance.

**Discovery hypothesis:** Understand how the Commission currently detects false recruitment information and how long it takes to verify and correct it.

**Potential pilot:** Weekly public-source information-integrity brief covering recruitment-related impersonation and false notices.

### 2. Bureau of Public Service Reforms (BPSR)
**Why relevant:** BPSR is focused on public-service reform, digital communication, citizen engagement and information management. Its 2026 programme specifically addressed AI-assisted communication, media monitoring and countering disinformation. citeturn0search6turn0search5

**Discovery hypothesis:** Determine whether cross-MDA intelligence about recurring communication failures and false information would support reform monitoring.

**Potential pilot:** Cross-MDA information-integrity trend brief.

### 3. Office of the Secretary to the Government of the Federation (OSGF)
**Why relevant:** OSGF has publicly warned about fake appointments and impersonation and publishes official reporting channels. citeturn0search2

**Discovery hypothesis:** Determine the operational burden of identifying and responding to impersonation incidents.

**Potential pilot:** Early-warning impersonation monitor for selected OSGF-related communications.

### 4. Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC)
**Why relevant:** NUPRC publicly documented fake and AI-generated employment letters and reported the matter to law enforcement. citeturn0search1

**Discovery hypothesis:** Understand whether the Commission needs continuous monitoring beyond individual public warnings.

**Potential pilot:** Recruitment/brand impersonation intelligence brief.

## Discovery questions

1. How do you currently discover false information using your institution's name?
2. Which channels are hardest to monitor?
3. Who verifies a suspected false notice?
4. How quickly can an official correction be issued?
5. Do you maintain an incident log or trend history?
6. What information would have been useful earlier in the last incident?
7. What existing tools already solve part of this problem?
8. Would an independent aggregated intelligence brief add value?
9. What would make the signal actionable rather than merely informative?
10. Would you prefer a weekly brief, dashboard, alert or API?

## Pilot proposal

**30 days. One information domain. One weekly intelligence brief.**

The pilot should measure:

- novel signals detected
- time from first signal to verification
- correction/response lag where observable
- false-positive rate
- buyer-reported usefulness
- decisions/actions influenced by the intelligence

## Commercial boundary

Problem Radar will not sell names, personal contact lists, identifiable complainants, private messages or individual behavioural profiles. The deliverable is aggregated intelligence with evidence provenance, confidence and trend context.

## Success gate

Do not build a large customer-facing platform until at least one target buyer confirms that the intelligence is **new, timely, actionable and worth paying for**.
