# Problem Radar — Product Architecture

## Two-sided system

Problem Radar should not be limited to a startup idea generator. The same evidence engine can support an intelligence business.

### Side A — Discover

Find recurring Nigerian problems, validate them, test manual solutions, and identify businesses worth building.

### Side B — Improve

Provide organizations with aggregated, privacy-preserving intelligence about recurring service failures, customer friction, access gaps, trust problems and emerging needs.

## Product surfaces

1. **Problem Radar Dashboard** — searchable evidence and trend maps.
2. **Agency Intelligence Reports** — recurring citizen/service pain by service category and geography where sufficiently supported.
3. **Business Customer Intelligence** — customer-friction and service-reliability signals.
4. **Sector Reports** — cross-company and cross-sector patterns.
5. **Insight API** — aggregated machine-readable signals for approved use cases.
6. **Opportunity Intelligence** — validated unmet-needs research for founders and innovation teams.

## Data boundary

The commercial product should sell insights, not people's identities. Raw personal data, identifiable complainants, private messages and sensitive records should not be exposed as a product. Processing and disclosure must have an appropriate lawful basis and privacy safeguards under Nigeria's data-protection framework.

## Flywheel

More evidence → better problem detection → better organizational insights → more validated interventions → better outcome data → stronger problem detection.

The system must preserve the distinction between observed evidence, inferred patterns and business hypotheses.
