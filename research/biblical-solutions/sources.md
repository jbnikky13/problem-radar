# Evidence and Sources

Core passages currently represented include:

- Genesis 41–47 — famine forecasting, reserves and distribution
- 2 Kings 4:1–7 — debt and productive asset activation
- Leviticus 19:9–10 — gleaning
- Leviticus 19:35–36 — honest weights and measures
- Deuteronomy 15 — debt release and economic reset
- Deuteronomy 19:15 — multiple witnesses
- Deuteronomy 24:14–15 — worker compensation
- Leviticus 25 — land, debt and economic reset/stewardship principles
- Isaiah 5:8 — excessive land/property accumulation
- 1 Kings 21 — abuse of political power and property
- Exodus 18:13–26 — distributed governance
- Matthew 18:15–17 — graduated dispute resolution
- Acts 2:44–45 — community resource sharing
- Acts 4:32–35 — community support

## Modern evidence sources to prioritize

Use current evidence independently from the biblical text.

Preferred source hierarchy:

1. UN / UN agencies
2. World Bank
3. WHO
4. FAO
5. UNHCR
6. UNCTAD

## Source discipline

When adding new entries:

- cite the exact biblical passage;
- distinguish quotation from interpretation;
- cite modern statistics separately;
- record publication date for time-sensitive data;
- avoid treating search snippets as primary evidence;
- update modern evidence when the atlas is used for investment or business decisions.
