# Modern Solution Concepts

These are hypotheses generated from the Biblical Solutions Atlas. They are **not validated businesses yet**.

## 1. TruthMark — digital trust infrastructure

**Problem:** Claims, credentials, products and metrics can be fabricated.

**Biblical mechanism:** Honest weights + truthful testimony + corroboration.

**Concept:** A verification layer that scores claims using evidence, provenance and independent corroboration.

**Potential customers:** Businesses, marketplaces, journalists, governments, financial institutions, AI platforms.

**MVP:** API that takes a claim/document and returns evidence, provenance and verification status.

---

## 2. FoodResilience — predictive food security

**Problem:** Food shortages and price shocks are often detected too late.

**Biblical mechanism:** Joseph's forecast/reserve/distribution system.

**Concept:** Predict local food shortages and connect surplus to deficit markets.

**MVP:** One-country food-risk dashboard using public market, weather and supply data.

---

## 3. DebtBridge — productive debt recovery

**Problem:** New credit can worsen existing debt.

**Biblical mechanism:** Widow's oil — activate productive capacity.

**Concept:** Map skills, assets and demand to a realistic debt-recovery plan.

**MVP:** Debt assessment + asset/skill inventory + income opportunity matching.

---

## 4. FairPay — worker payment protection

**Problem:** Workers can have difficulty proving or collecting owed compensation.

**Biblical mechanism:** Timely and fair payment of workers.

**Concept:** Contract/payment obligation tracking with evidence and dispute escalation.

**MVP:** Worker creates contract, records work, tracks payment deadline and generates an evidence package.

---

## 5. LandProof — property provenance

**Problem:** Land buyers may not know whether a seller has legitimate ownership.

**Biblical mechanism:** Protection of property/inheritance.

**Concept:** Aggregate property records, surveys, ownership history and dispute indicators into a pre-purchase risk report.

**MVP:** Verification workflow for one jurisdiction.

---

## 6. Resolve — dispute infrastructure

**Problem:** Small disputes can become expensive legal conflicts.

**Biblical mechanism:** Graduated reconciliation and escalation.

**Concept:** Structured negotiation → mediation → arbitration preparation.

**MVP:** Contract dispute intake + evidence timeline + settlement proposal generator.

---

## 7. Agent Governance Layer

**Problem:** Autonomous agents can act beyond intended authority.

**Biblical mechanism:** Delegated authority, accountability and escalation.

**Concept:** A policy engine that classifies AI actions by risk and applies ALLOW / APPROVAL / BLOCK / QUARANTINE controls.

**MVP:** SDK/API for pre-execution evaluation.

**Problem Radar adjacency:** IntentGuard.

---

## 8. Regeneration Ledger

**Problem:** Organizations report environmental impact without consistently connecting extraction to verified restoration.

**Biblical mechanism:** Stewardship and land rest.

**Concept:** Track resource extraction and verified regeneration.

**MVP:** One resource type, such as forestry or agriculture.

---

## 9. NewStart

**Problem:** Displaced people often lose access to proof of identity, skills and qualifications.

**Biblical mechanism:** Protection and inclusion of foreigners.

**Concept:** Portable, consent-based credential wallet.

**MVP:** Verified skill/qualification profile usable across participating employers or service providers.

---

## 10. Community Pool

**Problem:** People face emergency costs while communities have fragmented resources.

**Biblical mechanism:** Voluntary community sharing.

**Concept:** Transparent mutual-aid pools with verified need.

**MVP:** Closed community, school, workplace or professional association.

---

## 11. Evidence Graph

**Problem:** A single viral source can influence thousands of people.

**Biblical mechanism:** Multiple witnesses and testing of claims.

**Concept:** Build a graph connecting claims to sources, documents, timestamps and corroboration.

**MVP:** Browser/API claim verification tool.

---

## 12. Crisis Readiness Engine

**Problem:** Organizations prepare too late for predictable disruptions.

**Biblical mechanism:** Joseph's advance preparation.

**Concept:** Monitor leading indicators and generate preparation recommendations.

**MVP:** One sector, such as pharmacies, food distributors or small manufacturers.

---

# Priority candidates

Initial prioritization should consider:

- severity
- affected population
- willingness to pay
- technical feasibility
- regulatory complexity
- defensibility
- distribution difficulty
- time to MVP
- measurable outcome

No concept should be treated as validated until Problem Radar gathers modern evidence and customer interviews.
